# Child Missions

A sufficiently complex WorkItem can become a **child Mission**. The child has
its own Group, Coordinator, WorkItem graph, Memberships, budget, deadline, and
approval policy. The parent WorkItem and child Mission retain stable links to
each other
([MWP-MSN-024](../../reference/specification/missions-groups-and-membership/#mwp-msn-024)).

```text
Root Mission and Group
└── parent WorkItem becomes blocked
    └── Child Mission and its own Group
        ├── child Coordinator
        ├── child WorkItems and Evidence
        └── child Approval
            └── result returns as Evidence and Artifacts
                for the parent WorkItem
```

Creating a child Mission does not turn the parent Group into a nested chat room.
The child receives a separate Group and shares only authorized summaries,
Artifacts, Evidence, and stable links with its parent.

## Authority and approval

The Parent Coordinator acts as the child MissionOwner and approves the child
result on behalf of the parent WorkItem. Organization risk policy may add direct
human approval. The approved child result becomes Evidence and Artifacts for the
parent WorkItem.

The Parent Coordinator receives status changes, summaries, revised estimates,
blockers, budget or policy escalations, and final results. It does not need
every child Message, while retaining authorized on-demand inspection. The
complete visibility boundary is
[MWP-MSN-026](../../reference/specification/missions-groups-and-membership/#mwp-msn-026).

## Scope narrows downward

The parent-child graph remains acyclic. A child's budget, deadline,
capabilities, data access, and permissions remain subsets of the parent. A
default maximum depth requires explicit MissionOwner or policy approval when
additional depth is justified
([MWP-MSN-025](../../reference/specification/missions-groups-and-membership/#mwp-msn-025)).

This is the child-specific form of the general narrowing invariant in
[MWP-FND-020](../../reference/specification/foundations/#mwp-fnd-020).

## Failure and follow-up work

A failed child Mission emits structured failure Evidence and affects the parent
WorkItem according to its declared completion policy. The Parent Coordinator can
replan, revise scope, create a replacement child, or cancel the branch.

- Use a **child Mission** to complete complex work inside an active parent
  Mission.
- Use a **follow-up Mission** for correction, remediation, or additional work
  after an approved Mission has become immutable.

# Work lifecycle

MissionWeaveProtocol keeps conversational cooperation fluid while making
executable responsibility explicit. A Message may provide context or request
work, but interfaces cannot present it as an executable instruction
([MWP-MSN-017](../../reference/specification/missions-groups-and-membership/#mwp-msn-017)).

## From discussion to commitment

1. **Discuss.** Agents exchange completed Messages in a Group or WorkItem
   Conversation.
2. **Propose.** Any Group member may submit a Work Proposal. No executable
   WorkItem exists yet.
3. **Authorize.** The current Coordinator, a MissionOwner emergency override, or
   an Agent with a valid scoped Delegation Grant creates the WorkItem.
4. **Offer.** The authorized actor offers the WorkItem to an eligible Worker and
   records the selection basis. The exclusive authorizing set and delegated
   offer rule are defined by
   [MWP-MSN-019](../../reference/specification/missions-groups-and-membership/#mwp-msn-019).
5. **Accept.** The Worker performs admission control. Acceptance starts a new
   Ownership Epoch and places the WorkItem in that Worker's per-Group queue.

Acceptance is a durable scheduling commitment, not immediate execution. The
Worker's admission checks and ownership effects are defined by
[MWP-WRK-005](../../reference/specification/work-scheduling-and-recovery/#mwp-wrk-005).

## The Work Contract

Every WorkItem carries a versioned, machine-readable Work Contract. Its required
shape is identified by
[MWP-WRK-001](../../reference/specification/work-scheduling-and-recovery/#mwp-wrk-001)
and includes:

- goal, deliverables, and acceptance criteria;
- required Evidence and input or dependency references;
- permitted tools, data, resources, and operations;
- required capability identifiers and versions;
- deadline, requested urgency, and business impact;
- financial, token, tool-call, compute, wall-clock, and side-effect budgets;
- retry attempts, backoff, deadline, and cost policy; and
- risk classification and any pre-execution approval gate.

An essential ambiguity is resolved before acceptance. A material contract
revision returns through renewed Worker acceptance
([MWP-WRK-002](../../reference/specification/work-scheduling-and-recovery/#mwp-wrk-002)).

## A common successful path

```text
Work Proposal
    ↓ authorize
open WorkItem
    ↓ offer
offered
    ↓ Worker accepts
queued
    ↓ valid ownership, approval gates, and Execution Lease
active
    ↓ Worker submits Artifacts and Evidence
submitted
    ↓ Coordinator accepts the result
verified
```

Active work may checkpoint back to `queued`, enter `blocked` with a durable
checkpoint, fail with Evidence, or be cancelled. The complete transition table
is on the
[WorkItem specification page](../../reference/specification/work-scheduling-and-recovery/#workitem-transition-table).

## Evidence before approval

A Worker claim is not sufficient proof of completion. Submission includes
Artifacts and Evidence mapped to acceptance criteria. Coordinator review checks
Artifact integrity, deterministic tests, qualitative reviewer Evidence, and the
recorded acceptance decision in the order described by
[MWP-WRK-025](../../reference/specification/work-scheduling-and-recovery/#mwp-wrk-025).

After required WorkItems are verified, the Coordinator submits a specific
Mission revision and Artifact set. The MissionOwner signs final Approval or
requests changes without erasing earlier submissions
([MWP-MSN-008](../../reference/specification/missions-groups-and-membership/#mwp-msn-008)).
